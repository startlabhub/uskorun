# USKORUN — 30/60/90-day Research & Pilot Roadmap
_Date: 2026-10-15. Status: PROPOSED._

## Days 0–30: validation
- Select **one California pilot market** based on existing relationships; geography TBD.
- 20–30 runner discovery interviews: current routine, challenge participation, desired value, consent comfort.
- 10–15 merchant interviews: gross margin, desired new-customer acquisition cost, discount capacity, redemption workflow.
- 3–5 running-club interviews: attendance, fees, rules, brand concerns.
- Prototype landing and signup intent; do not imply Apple/Strava integration.
- Legal review for contests, charity solicitations, privacy, promotions, discount conditions.
- Document competitor matrix and complete Deep Research import after that separate report is available.
**Gate 1:** >=2 organizers and >=5 merchants confirm specific pilot dates/offers in writing.

## Days 31–60: controlled pilot
- Develop minimum web app or no-code operational prototype with QA evidence.
- Run 2–3 club events, supervised check-in, proof of participation policies.
- Issue non-cash low-risk offers with clear expiry/stock/conditions.
- Instrument funnel: event view → join → attendance → qualified → issue → redemption.
- Monitor fraud/duplicate redemption, complaints, user feedback and merchant experience.
- For distance-based rewards, obtain credible independent proof before automatic awarding.
**Gate 2:** Demonstrate functioning redemption, participation and enough data to estimate merchant ROI (not just clicks).

## Days 61–90: repeatability
- Repeat with the same clubs and merchants; evaluate re-enrollment and partner renewal.
- Test two reward economics: fixed merchant offer vs sponsor-funded event.
- Build merchant dashboard and automated settlement only after finance and legal validation.
- Decide whether richer distance evidence is worth cost and privacy complexity.
- Review platform agreements: Strava requires independent compliance review; Apple Health requires native iOS and dedicated legal sign-off.
**Gate 3:** Merchant renewals, repeat runners, controlled fraud loss and economically credible acquisition cost.

| Partnerships | Signed pilot terms and redemption procedures | Why merchant pays |
| Engineering | Verification and voucher engine | Can be operated safely |
| Legal/privacy | Approved promotion design and privacy notice | Can launch compliantly |
| Growth | Club referrals and co-marketing tests | Acquisition viability |
| Analytics | Event definitions / funnel / unit economics | Continue or pivot |

## Explicit no-go triggers
- Only viable with unapproved Strava data usage.
- Apple Health data monetized or shared for merchant targeting.
- Merchants cannot make or reliably redeem offers.
- Fraud cost exceeds viable reward value.
- Repetition depends on unsustainable cash subsidies.

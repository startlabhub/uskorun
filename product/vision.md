# USKORUN — Product Vision (v0.1)
_Date: 2026-10-15. Status: HYPOTHESIS; validation required._

## One-liner
USKORUN turns verified local running participation into meaningful rewards, community access, and optional philanthropic impact — without becoming another general-purpose running tracker.

## Core loops
1. Runner discovers a nearby challenge or a club event.
2. Runner joins and consents to **one specific verification method**.
3. Event organizer / acceptable evidence confirms participation and, where applicable, a qualifying distance.
4. USKORUN issues a single-use reward claim with expiration and merchant-specific terms.
5. Merchant redeems it and sees aggregated, non-sensitive campaign reporting.
6. Runner has reason to return for another event and reward.

## Personas / jobs-to-be-done (HYPOTHESIS)
- **Casual runner:** social motivation, a reachable goal and a small tangible incentive.
- **Running club organizer:** attendance, community growth, repeat participation, sponsorship.
- **Neighborhood merchant:** measurable visits / redemptions / incremental sales, not raw wellness data.
- **Corporate wellness buyer (later):** team challenge engagement; legal and privacy review first.
- **Charity partner (later):** clearly disclosed optional donations or sponsored charitable outcomes; no unsupported promises.

## Product bets
- B1: Local, real-world rewards will drive repeat participation more than generic digital badges alone.
- B2: Clubs can acquire runners much more efficiently than paid consumer ads at launch.
- B3: Merchants will fund offers if they can measure redemption and incremental footfall.
- B4: Event check-ins are enough to validate initial product-market fit; universal automatic tracking is **not** essential for MVP.
- B5: USKORUN's differentiation is an offline merchant network + community, not proprietary running statistics.

## MVP positioning
**“Run with your club. Unlock local rewards.”**
Do **not** claim integration with Apple Health or Strava until it is working and legally permitted.

## Revenue hypotheses (not forecasts)
- Merchant campaign subscription or fixed placement **only where independently lawful**, with transparent labeling.
- Redemption-based merchant fee (check merchant margins and payment/affiliate terms).
- Club management SaaS add-on, if clubs demonstrate willingness to pay.
- Co-sponsored community campaigns (with clear sponsor disclosures).
- Premium user subscription is a later-stage test, not the base case.

## Non-goals for launch
No leaderboard cash prizes; no wagers; no marketplace for user health data; no sale of GPS location trails; no native iOS app by default; no Strava-dependent architecture.

## North-star candidate (test, don't assume)
Monthly **qualifying challenge participants who redeem at least one valid reward**. Guardrails: fraud rate, genuine repeat participation, merchant renewal, user opt-outs and complaints.

## Decision gates
- Problem evidence: 20–30 runner interviews, 3–5 clubs, 10–15 merchants.
- Pilot: >=2 clubs + >=5 merchants willing to run a time-limited experiment.
- Technical: demonstrated valid issuance, redemption and auditability.
- Compliance: documented approval for each external data use and promotion mechanic.
- Business: positive value creation for both runner and participating merchant after discount cost.

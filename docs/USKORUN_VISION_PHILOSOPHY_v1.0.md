# USKORUN — Vision & Philosophy v1.0

**Date:** 2026-10-28  
**Status:** Founder-approved product direction (15/15 discovery decisions)  
**Market:** Greater Sacramento, California  
**Brand:** Independent sports technology startup

## Mission
Turn everyday movement into real opportunities for people, communities, and local businesses.

**Tagline:** Move more. Connect more. Get rewarded.

USKORUN is not merely a running tracker or coupon directory. It is a local movement-and-rewards ecosystem connecting active residents, groups, and neighborhood businesses.

## Four equal pillars
1. **Reward:** Savings and tangible local rewards.
2. **Community:** Belonging, friends, teams, and shared goals.
3. **Challenge:** Competition, personal progress, and achievement.
4. **Discover:** Explore local businesses and neighborhood places.

**Core principle:** Your movement creates value — for you, your team, and your city.

## Product and participants
- Initial marketing focus: adults **25–55**, with registration available to adults **18+**.
- Launch geography: Sacramento, Roseville, Rocklin, Folsom, Elk Grove, and surrounding Greater Sacramento.
- Both **running and walking** earn rewards under **distinct earning rules**; coefficients and anti-abuse thresholds to be decided in the MVP specification.
- MVP tracks activity with **in-app GPS**; Strava, Apple Health, Garmin and other integrations come later.
- A mobile web app is the first delivery channel. Background GPS operation when the screen locks must be tested early; do not promise reliable recording until verified on target mobile devices.

## Hybrid rewards
1. Activity points, awarded for validated running/walking sessions.
2. Sponsor-backed challenges with predefined goals and rewards.
3. Instant offers from approved local partners.
4. Rewards redeemable using a **QR code** or a **one-time promo code**, with unique identity, expiry, and server-side one-time redemption.
5. No sponsor reward can be published until sponsor terms and reward availability are confirmed.

## Social and growth mechanics
- Personal progress, achievements and streaks.
- Local leaderboards, with fair separation of running and walking.
- Teams/clubs and group challenges.
- **Community goals:** a group accumulates mileage during a defined period; reaching a threshold unlocks a predefined group benefit.
- Goals can additionally require a minimum number of genuinely active participants, motivating invitations rather than excessive mileage from a few athletes.
- Members choose which challenges to join; participation in multiple challenges is supported.
- Any user may create an ordinary group or challenge. Public sponsor-backed rewards are subject to USKORUN moderation and sponsor confirmation.
- One-click sharing to social networks and personalized invite links.
- Referral bonuses for inviter and invitee only after the invited user completes a verified first GPS activity.
- Team competitions and shared-goal rewards reinforce referral growth.
- Fraud prevention and privacy controls are essential; no purchasing leaderboard advantage.

## Business model
**Freemium + B2B.**
- Users: free GPS activity, basic points, rewards, teams, challenges; optional premium capabilities to be defined and priced later.
- Businesses: free basic partner listing and baseline offers; paid promotion, sponsored challenges, optional analytics/services.
- Measure issued and redeemed rewards, validated visits where technically supportable, and repeat business participation.
- Bloggers/influencers act as **business-acquisition partners**, paid commission on signed and paid business contracts; compensation structure remains open.
- USKORUN is **independent** of USKO Realty. USKO or other local companies may participate as sponsors on ordinary commercial terms; no automatic affiliation is implied.

## Launch strategy
### Phase 1 — closed beta
**50–100 testers.** Validate GPS quality, running vs walking rules, point earning, teams, invitations, QR/promo redemptions, partner workflow and basic retention. Do not scale advertising before critical user paths work.

### Phase 2 — public city challenge
Target **1,000 active users**, not merely registrations, through one citywide challenge uniting running clubs, local bloggers/influencers and business sponsors.

**Working campaign concept:** “Sacramento Moves Together / 10,000 Miles Together.” The 10,000-mile goal, sponsor counts, and incentives are illustrative and require operational validation.

A meaningful active user has at least one valid completed GPS activity during the chosen measurement window. Final reporting window and KPIs to be specified.

## Suggested North Star and metrics
**North Star candidate:** Weekly active participants with at least one verified running/walking session (to validate during beta).

Track activation (first verified session), week-1 and day-30 retention, referral-to-active conversion, challenge participation, reward redemption, partner campaign renewal and fraud rate.

## Responsible reward design
- Prefer guaranteed benefits unlocked by clear shared goals.
- Do **not** include randomized prize drawings in MVP without California-specific legal review.
- Avoid paid lotteries; any future sweepstakes must have legally reviewed eligibility and no-purchase entry rules.
- Protect personal location data, explain consent, and minimize storage and exposure of precise routes.
- Published rewards must be real, accurately described, funded/approved, and reliably redeemable.

## MVP product principles
1. **Engagement before scale:** Prove repeated behavior with 50–100 people.
2. **Real rewards only:** Fail closed on unconfirmed offers.
3. **Simple journey:** Sign up → join a challenge → move → earn/redeem → invite a friend.
4. **Trustworthy activity:** Validate GPS, flag anomalies, prevent duplicate earning/redemption.
5. **Mobile first:** Design for phone browsers and test tracking reliability before launch.

## Implementation sequence
1. Founder interview and philosophy — **complete (15/15)**.
2. MVP specification: roles, screens, flows, activity rules, rewards economics, partner moderation, anti-fraud, schemas, privacy and acceptance criteria.
3. Build mobile-web prototype.
4. Deploy to Railway, set up environments, monitoring and QA.
5. Closed beta, fixes and measured public release.

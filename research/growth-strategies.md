# Acquisition and Growth Experiments — USKORUN
_As of 2026-10-15. Status: test proposals, not claimed competitor tactics._

## Channel ladder
1. **Run clubs as distribution partners**: co-host recurring meetups. Supply organizer QR, co-branded event pages, club-specific links, rewards.
2. **Running retailers and local businesses**: sponsor offers for events that already attract the appropriate customer.
3. **Events/races**: partner with existing local 5K organizers; distribute only by permission.
4. **Referral loops**: club members invite friends to a specific future event, not to a generic app.
5. **Local search and Google Business Profiles**: event-specific SEO pages with location and accurate date/time; never invent events.
6. **Creators/micro-influencers**: UGC showcasing an actual club event and disclosed sponsor; measure incremental signups.
7. **Paid social** only after repeat engagement; small controlled spend with holdout/geographic attribution.

## Trackable funnel
Qualified local impression → event page view → signup → attendance → eligible → voucher issued → voucher redeemed → second event joined → second event attended.

Capture channel using consent-compliant UTM and organizer-specific referral links. Merchants see only campaign aggregates, not user workouts or protected health information.

## Early experiments
| Test | Variant | Primary metric | Guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Club recruitment | Organizers invited personally vs merchant-introduced | Pilot club acceptance | Organizer burden |
| Message | Saving money vs community | Actual attendance/signup | Refund/complaints |
| Reward type | Fixed item vs % off | Redeemed and incremental purchases | Merchant gross margin |
| Redemption | Staff QR scan vs short code | Successful, fast redemption | Fraud and customer friction |
| Organic distribution | Club landing pages vs social-only signup | Qualified attendees | Attribution quality |
| Repeat | Monthly challenge vs weekly habit | 30-day second attendance | Excess messages |

## CAC discipline
Do not compare paid ad **clicks** with organic **attendees**. Use paid CAC per verified retained participant, include incentive subsidy / support / fraud. Partner acquisition cost includes labor and event support.

## Why not buy users at launch?
Paid consumer ads can create coupon-hunters rather than a running habit. Establish product-market fit through communities first.

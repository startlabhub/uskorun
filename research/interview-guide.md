# USKORUN — Customer Discovery Interview Guide
_Date: 2026-10-15. Research protocol draft._

## Runner interviews (20–30)
Ask about the last 3 running sessions before suggesting our product.
1. When/where did you run last week and why?
2. Do you use a club/event? How did you first join?
3. What caused you to attend a second time?
4. What happened when you tried to complete a challenge?
5. What rewards, if any, have ever changed your participation?
6. Would you use a merchant perk if redeeming took 30 seconds? Which local merchants?
7. Is organizer check-in acceptable, or do you expect GPS validation?
8. What data would you refuse to share for a discount?
9. Would club status/access or a charitable contribution be more valuable than a discount?
10. Invite them to a real pilot without offering leading inducements.

## Club organizers (3–5)
1. How many attendees per event and what is repeat attendance?
2. How do you verify attendees? Is distance actually verified?
3. Are clubs willing to be the distribution channel or only a listed location?
4. What operational burden and fraud are unacceptable?
5. What types of sponsor terms are already accepted?
6. Would a referral URL / QR and merchant offers help recruitment?
7. Who can approve data and photo usage?
8. Will you commit to a dated pilot?

## Merchants (10–15)
1. Walk through a recent promotion that actually worked.
2. What is your contribution margin after discounts?
3. Which customer segment would be incremental?
4. Can employees verify a QR voucher at checkout?
5. Maximum coupon inventory / expense cap?
6. Would you pay a flat fee, per redemption or sponsorship? What budget line?
7. What reporting convinces you to run a second campaign?
8. What data and consent do you actually need?
9. Potential conflicts with existing promotions and POS?
10. Will you sign specific written terms?

## Research hygiene
Avoid leading hypothetical yes/no questions. Record observed actions, quotes, source/date, sample composition, objections and permission for follow-up. Distinguish donated/discounted goods from paid media spend.

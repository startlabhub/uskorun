# USKORUN — Merchant, Club and Donation Models
_Date: 2026-10-15. Status: FINANCIAL / COMMERCIAL HYPOTHESES._

## Partner value proposition
A sponsor receives a defined, measurable experiment with eligible local runners. USKORUN issues the right to claim a perk; the merchant scans a one-time token; the dashboard reports campaign-level activity **without exposing workouts, exact location trails, or private health data**.

## Potential merchant offers (examples, not signed deals)
- Free low-cost item with a qualifying in-store purchase.
- Fixed-price bundle for club participants.
- Limited number of $-off vouchers with clear minimum spend.
- Percentage discount on selected items; merchant controls cap, conditions, staff instructions.
- Sponsored club refreshments / event access.
Avoid wagering, prize lotteries or “pay to win” economics; legal review required for promotions.

## Simple pilot contract outline
- Campaign ID, term, number of vouchers, benefit and exclusions.
- Responsible party for discount cost and for payment processor fees.
- Redemption rules, opening times, expiry, geographic scope, handling returns.
- Permission to use marks, logos and event photos (separate consents).
- Aggregated reporting and data processing / privacy provisions.
- Customer support, dispute workflow and opt-out / early cancellation.
- Fraud and abuse allocation, liability cap and insurance requirements as appropriate.
- No right for merchants to access raw athlete activity or protected location/HealthKit records.

## Merchant revenue options to test separately
A. Setup/management fee per campaign, including staff training.
B. Monthly recurring dashboard and limited campaigns.
C. Per verified redemption fee, if the merchant can absorb it.
D. Community sponsorship/event underwriting.
Do not double count the same discount as both merchant spend and platform revenue.

## Club models
- Free club event listing and RSVP, paid club admin features later.
- Organization-supported perks with no member fee.
- Donation-supported club membership / free access unlocked by attendance: separate product experiments, clarify beneficiary, destination, refunds and taxes.

## Donations and charitable claims
Do not label money as a “donation to charity” without an actual eligible nonprofit partner, verified beneficiary, enforceable flow and reviewed U.S. fundraising rules. If a user gives money to USKORUN, that is **not necessarily a tax-deductible charitable donation**. Avoid sweepstakes or contest mechanics linked to payment until legal review. Disclose exactly who receives funds and when.

## Merchant interview checklist
1. Average gross margin on proposed products?
2. Maximum discount without destroying contribution margin?
3. Estimated incremental purchases, not just coupon claims?
4. Can staff redeem via simple phone web page?
5. Is the target new customers, repeat customers, or community branding?
6. What data is required for monthly renewal decision?
7. What budget could be approved for a 30-day event campaign?
8. Does the business have legal/brand restrictions on promotions?
9. Any operational problems at peak times?
10. Will they sign a specific pilot offer and allow a measurement holdout?

## Success criteria
Renewal or expansion intention backed by economics, not just positive interview sentiment. Measure voucher issue, redemption, incremental transactions where possible, average discount burden, staff effort, disputes and repeat patronage.
